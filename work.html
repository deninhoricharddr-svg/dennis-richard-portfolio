Analyzing the effects of oil prices on the exchange rate in Malawi.

My undergraduate economics dissertation at the Catholic University of Malawi investigated international oil prices and the Malawi exchange rate using applied time-series methods, including an autoregressive distributed lag framework.

Research focus

External price shocks, exchange-rate dynamics and implications for macroeconomic analysis.
