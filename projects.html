THE STANDARD

How projects earn a place here.

01 — A genuine question

Each project starts with a business, economic or financial decision and a documented data source.

02 — Verifiable methods

SQL, notebooks, statistical syntax, models and documented validation procedures where appropriate.

03 — Honest findings

Clearly distinguish observed evidence from scenarios, assumptions, projections and limitations.

04 — A decision-ready outcome

Every finished study includes an executive summary, practical conclusions and reproducibility notes.
